# Rules for the Laboratory

### What information must never be published.
- System Credentials
- API Keys
- Real names, addresses.
- Audits of real system


### How screenshots will be reviewed and sanitized
- The screenshots will be reviewed against the information that have been decided must not be published. Sensitive information will be cropped out, replaced or retracted with dark pen.

### How fictional usernames and organization names will be used
- Be used on all assets, accounts created solely for the lab.


### Where passwords and secrets will be stored
- Best know to the system administrator. They are obligated to keep properly and make available only when they are sure it is needed to proper personnels.

### Which systems you are authorized to administer
- All systems and assets.

### Why the lab must remain separated from real production environments
- The real production environments are entirely two different scopes of administration therefore the first reason is permissions.
- They each possess different impacts.
- Human error could make put the production environments as collaterals.
