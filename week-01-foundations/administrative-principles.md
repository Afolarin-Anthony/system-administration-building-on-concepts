# Administrative Principles

The following principles guide all decisions made throughout this course.

## Principle 1: Know What Exists

Unknown assets cannot be managed effectively.

## Principle 2: Standardize Everything Practical

Consistency reduces complexity and troubleshooting effort.

## Principle 3: Verify Before Assuming

Evidence is preferred over intuition.

## Principle 4: Document Decisions

Future administrators should understand both the implementation and the reasoning.

## Principle 5: Protect Least Privilege

Access should be granted only when required.

## Principle 6: Plan for Failure

Every system will eventually fail.

## Principle 7: Observe Continuously

Monitoring is essential for informed administration.

## Principle 8: Automate Repetitive Work

Consistency improves reliability.

## Principle 9: Control Change

Every change introduces risk.

## Principle 10: Recovery Matters More Than Backups

A backup is only valuable if restoration succeeds.
