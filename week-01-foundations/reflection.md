## Why is inventory more than a list of devices?
- An inventory explains how each device in their respective capacity all offer different means in achieving a requirement. Therefore
they should be were audited.

## What is the difference between an assumption and a verified fact?
- An assumption is an administrator making a conclusion without having evidence while a verified fact is one that has been tested across a number of variables.

## Why should requirements be defined before selecting tools?
- To ensure we have the exact number of tools and the right tools needed.
